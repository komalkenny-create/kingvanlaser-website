KingVan KV-UV15 Achieves CE Certification as European Thermal Strip Sector Adopts UV Laser Marking for EN 14024 Compliance

July 29, 2026

JINAN, China – KingVan Technology has received CE certification for its KV-UV15 ultraviolet laser marking system, clearing the machine for unrestricted sale across the European Economic Area. The certification—awarded following comprehensive testing at a TÜV SÜD-accredited laboratory in Munich—validates the KV-UV15's compliance with EU machinery safety directive 2006/42/EC, electromagnetic compatibility directive 2014/30/EU, and the Restriction of Hazardous Substances (RoHS) 2011/65/EU. With the CE mark secured, KingVan is positioning to capture a significant share of the rapidly expanding European market for thermal break strip laser marking systems.

The CE certification milestone arrives at a pivotal moment for the European window profile industry. European fenestration manufacturers are increasingly consolidating their polyamide thermal break strip marking processes around UV laser technology as the preferred method for achieving EN 14024 compliance—the European standard that governs the performance and traceability marking requirements for thermally broken aluminum profiles. The standard requires permanent, legible marking identifying material composition and batch origin at intervals not exceeding 300 mm along each strip, a requirement that industry participants have found increasingly difficult to satisfy with inkjet or mechanical embossing methods as production speeds continue to rise.

"CE certification gives European buyers the full regulatory confidence to specify KingVan's UV marking systems without qualification," said KingVan's director of European operations. "For profile extruders in Germany, Italy, Spain, and Poland, the CE mark means the KV-UV15 meets every safety and environmental requirement they need to satisfy their own insurers and end customers. Combined with EN 14024 compliance validation that our application engineers can demonstrate on-site, the machine has quickly become the preferred capital investment for extruders expanding or upgrading their marking capacity."

European Market Adoption and Installation Milestones

KingVan's European sales pipeline has grown steadily throughout 2026, with the company now reporting 23 KV-UV15 systems installed or in the commissioning process across seven EU member states. Italy—home to the largest concentration of premium aluminum window profile extruders in Europe—accounts for the largest single-country deployment with nine systems installed in the provinces of Brescia, Padua, and Naples. Germany follows with six systems serving major extruders in the Rhineland and Bavaria regions, where EN 14024 compliance enforcement has been most rigorous following the German Energy Saving Ordinance (EnEV) updates.

"The European market approaches thermal strip marking very differently from what we see in Asia or the Middle East," the European director noted. "European extruders are typically family-owned Mittelstand companies with 50 to 200 employees, running 6 to 15 extrusion lines in highly automated facilities. They value reliability, service support, and integration with existing plant-floor IT systems above raw speed. Our MarkOS enterprise software—which provides full batch tracking, production reporting, and SAP/ERP integration—has been a decisive differentiator in European tenders, often tipping the purchasing decision in our favor even when competing against established European laser marking brands."

KingVan has also established a European spare parts and service hub in Bologna, Italy, stocking KV-UV laser source modules, galvanometer scanner assemblies, and control electronics for the entire installed base. The hub is staffed by two application engineers and a service technician, all of whom have completed factory training at KingVan's Jinan headquarters. The company has committed to a 24-hour on-site response time for any European KV-UV customer experiencing a production-stopping equipment issue.

Performance Data from European Production Environments

Production data collected from KV-UV15 installations operating in European extrusion facilities provides compelling validation of the system's performance claims. At a major Italian extruder's facility in Padua, a single KV-UV15 has been marking PA66 GF25 thermal strips continuously for 3,200 operating hours since installation in March 2026, with zero unscheduled downtime. The system marks Data Matrix codes and alphanumeric product identifiers at a sustained throughput of 135 strips per minute—slightly below the theoretical maximum of 150 per minute due to the extruder's line speed—with 100% readability verified by the facility's downstream vision inspection station.

At a German installation serving a manufacturer of premium curtain wall profiles for commercial building projects, the KV-UV15 has demonstrated the ability to switch between six different marking formats—corresponding to six distinct thermal strip product specifications—in under 200 milliseconds per changeover, enabling full mixed-model production without interrupting line flow. The installation's integrated vision alignment system has eliminated the operator supervision requirement entirely; the facility reports that a single technician now monitors three extrusion lines simultaneously, compared to one operator per line under the previous inkjet marking system.

"The CE certification validation gives us the confidence to offer European customers a full-process guarantee," the director of European operations added. "We commit that the KV-UV15 will mark any EN 14024-compliant polyamide thermal strip profile at the rated throughput with 99.5% uptime, or KingVan will provide a replacement system at no cost during the warranty period. That is the level of assurance that European Mittelstand companies expect from their capital equipment suppliers, and we are proud to deliver it."

Next Steps: KV-UV20 European Launch

Building on the KV-UV15's European market reception, KingVan has announced that its higher-throughput KV-UV20 system—capable of marking up to 200 strips per minute—is scheduled to begin CE certification testing in September 2026, with European availability targeted for Q1 2027. The KV-UV20 incorporates a 20-watt pulsed UV laser source and an expanded-format galvanometer scan head that enables a marking field of 200 × 200 mm, accommodating wider thermal strip profiles used in premium curtain wall and structural glazing applications.

KingVan will showcase both the KV-UV15 and the KV-UV20 prototype at BAU 2027 in Munich—Europe's premier trade fair for architecture, materials, and systems—where the company will occupy a dedicated exhibition space in the aluminum processing hall. Early registrations for private demonstrations at the show have already reached 40% of the available booking capacity, reflecting the European fenestration industry's accelerating interest in UV laser marking technology.

"Europe represents KingVan's single largest strategic growth opportunity outside of China," the European director concluded. "The region's rigorous regulatory framework, high labor costs, and premium quality expectations make it the ideal market for a precision UV laser marking system like the KV-UV15. With CE certification secured, our European service infrastructure in place, and a growing installed base that validates our technology, we are on track to exceed 50 installed systems in Europe by the end of 2027."
